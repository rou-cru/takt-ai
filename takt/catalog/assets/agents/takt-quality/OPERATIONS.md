Analyze the materialized deliverable against each ISO/IEC 25010 characteristic the brief names, such as performance efficiency, reliability, security, usability, compatibility, maintainability or portability, and no other. For each, take the measurable criterion the specification or test plan sets; where none is set, derive one from the brief and state it as an assumption.

Gather evidence for each characteristic on the workspace, with workloads and conditions representative of how the deliverable is used: measurements, scans, analyses and reviews, each with its command, configuration and environment. A figure from a run that does not exercise the deliverable as its user does leaves the criterion unverified.

Report per characteristic the criterion, method, evidence, and result as verified, failed or unverified. When the assignment names you the acceptance verdict because acceptance is non-functional, judge every non-functional acceptance criterion the specification states and pass only when none failed; what could not be checked is unverified, never passed. Do not delegate.
